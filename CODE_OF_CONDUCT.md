# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Orca should be a professional, practical, and welcoming project. Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still asking for clarity when needed.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, docs, and behavior.
- Accept feedback without hostility.
- Make space for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, threats, sustained disruption, unwanted sexual attention, discriminatory language, or publishing private information without permission.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, release channels, and any other community venue connected to Orca.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, block users, or take other reasonable action when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

To report a concern, contact the maintainers privately through the security contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled with discretion.
